Dr. Xiaohan Zhang

Associate Professor · Fudan University

I work at the System Software & Security Lab, Fudan University. My research focuses on cybersecurity, including mobile application security, malware detection, and applied AI security. I obtained my B.Eng and Ph.D. at Fudan University under the supervision of Prof. Min Yang.

复旦大学系统软件与安全实验室青年副研究员。 研究方向为网络安全，包括移动应用安全、恶意代码检测与应用 AI 安全。 本科与博士均就读于复旦大学，导师为杨珉教授。

I have published papers as (co-)first author at the "Big Four" security venues, and received the NDSS 2025 Distinguished Paper Award, the USENIX Security 2022 Distinguished Paper Award, the USENIX Security 2025 Honorable Mention Award, and a CCS 2020 Distinguished Paper Award Nomination. My work on face verification security won the CNVD 2021 Most Valuable Vulnerability from the Chinese National Vulnerability Database.

以（共同）第一作者身份在安全领域“四大顶会”发表论文，获得 NDSS 2025 杰出论文奖、USENIX Security 2022 杰出论文奖、 USENIX Security 2025 Honorable Mention Award 以及 CCS 2020 杰出论文提名；人脸认证安全方面的研究获中国国家漏洞库（CNVD） 2021 最具价值漏洞。

I teach courses on deep learning foundations, system security, optimizing compilers, and attack-and-defense techniques. Students under my supervision have won the First Prize of the "Huawei Cup" China Postgraduate Network Security Innovation Competition, the Gold Award of DCIC 2025 (App Security Track), and the Second Prize of the National College Student Information Security Competition (2026), and their research projects have been funded by the School of Cybersecurity's Student Innovation Program (one concluded as "Excellent", top 5%).

主讲深度学习基础、系统安全、高级优化编译、高级攻防技术等课程。 指导学生获“华为杯”中国研究生网络安全创新大赛一等奖、 数字中国创新大赛（DCIC 2025）应用安全赛道金奖、 全国大学生信息安全竞赛作品赛二等奖（2026）； 指导的学生科研项目获国家网络安全学院创新计划资助，其中一项结题获评“优秀”（前 5%）。

I am always looking for highly-motivated undergraduate students interested in cybersecurity. Feel free to reach out at xh_zhang [AT] fudan.edu.cn.

常年招收有志于网络安全、自我驱动力强的本科生， 联系邮箱：xh_zhang [AT] fudan.edu.cn。
